## Description

Tempo is a focused personal timekeeper built for clear sessions and useful history.

Start several timers when your work overlaps. Pause, resume, stop, or edit a running timer without losing its lifecycle. Organize entries with nested folders and reusable labels, add notes, and review daily, weekly, and monthly patterns.

Your account and timers stay synchronized across the Tempo website, iPhone, and Mac. Home Screen and desktop widgets keep the current timer and recent totals visible without opening the app.

Tempo includes:

- Concurrent running and paused timers
- Editable active timers and completed entries
- Manual time entries
- Nested folders and reusable labels
- Day, week, and month summaries
- Live synchronization across devices
- iPhone and Mac widgets
- Sign in with Apple, Google, or email through Auth0
- In-app account deletion

Tempo has no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## App Review notes

Tempo requires an account because timers synchronize through Convex across web, iPhone, Mac, and widgets. Sign in with Apple, Google, and email are presented through Auth0 Universal Login.

To delete an account, open Setup, choose Delete My Account, and confirm. This permanently deletes the Auth0 identity and all associated Tempo profile, timer, entry, folder, and label data.

The app supports iPhone. The embedded widget extension intentionally declares the same iPhone-only device family as its containing app.

Reviewer credentials must be added in App Store Connect immediately before submission and must remain active throughout review.

Target audience: individuals, students, freelancers, and knowledge workers who want a private record of how they spend their time. Tempo supports concurrent timers, editable active sessions, completed entries, folders, labels, summaries, and cross-device synchronization.

Access instructions: launch Tempo, choose Continue to Tempo, and use the reviewer credentials in App Store Connect. No sample files, purchase, or subscription are required. The Now tab contains the main timer flow; account deletion is under Setup.

External services: Auth0 provides Apple, Google, and email authentication. Convex provides application data storage and live synchronization. Tempo does not use payment processors, advertising, analytics, or AI services for its core functionality.

Tempo has the same functionality in every region. It has no paid content, public user-generated content, content reporting or blocking features, sensitive device permission prompts, regulated services, or protected third-party material.

Before every submission, add a public screen-recording URL and the physical iPhone model and current iOS version used for the final test. The recording must begin with a fresh app launch and demonstrate authentication, starting, editing, pausing, resuming and stopping a timer, viewing the log, adding a widget, and the account-deletion flow.

## App privacy answers

Tempo does not track users and does not use data for third-party advertising, developer advertising, or marketing.

Data linked to the user and used for app functionality:

Auth0 processes authentication information. Convex stores and synchronizes Tempo application data.
